How I Build.
From Concept to Final Delivery.

Every project follows a disciplined 4-stage creative pipeline. Zero guesswork, radical craft, and transparent milestones from initial brief to multi-medium launch.

Phase 01

Discovery & Direction

Unpacking your event ethos, audience expectations, and core narrative. We map visual tension, curated moodboards, and chromatic benchmarks before touching pixel one.

  • + Cultural & Visual Research
  • + Curated Creative Moodboards
  • + Strategic Aesthetic Direction

Phase 02
grid_view

Grid & Architecture

Engineering the mathematical scaffolding of the canvas. Calibrating bilingual Ethiopic Fidel with modern Latin grotesques, establishing 12-column grids, and testing chromatic contrast.

  • + Bilingual Typographic System
  • + 12-Column Modular Grid
  • + Calibrated Chromatic Palette
Milestone: System Architecture

Phase 03
movie_edit

High-Fidelity Craft

Rigorous composition across Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere. Layering tactile grain, fine optical kerning, subpixel alignment, and cinematic film LUT grading for motion assets.

  • + Master Key Art Posters
  • + Cinematic Film Color Grade
  • + Motion Teasers & Intro Loops
Milestone: Full Campaign Reveal

Phase 04

Deployment & Launch

Exporting production-ready deliverables calibrated for every touchpoint: large-format 300 DPI CMYK print flyers, 9:16 vertical motion reels, social feeds, and master source archives.

  • + Master Source Archives (PSD/AI)
